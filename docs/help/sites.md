# Your sites

Each site has its own name, its own address on levuche.page and its own
teammates. You can have up to 5 sites.

## Make your first site

1. Sign in. If you have no site yet, you go straight to **Make a site**.
2. Give it a name. You can change it later.
3. Pick its address: 3 to 30 lowercase letters and numbers, with single
   hyphens between them, like `sam-rivera`. Your site will be at
   `sam-rivera.levuche.page`.
4. Choose **Make the site**.

If the address is taken, pick another; your site name stays filled in.

## Make another site

On a site's page, or on the list of your sites, choose **Make another
site**.

## Switch between sites

Choose the site's name at the top, next to the Levuche logo, then pick
another site. With more than one site, the Levuche logo opens the list of
them.

## Rename a site or change its picture

On the site's page, choose **Name and picture**.

## Work with teammates

On the site's page, choose **Teammates** to invite people and choose what
they can do:

- **Admin**: everything, including teammates and billing.
- **Editor**: change the site.
- **Viewer**: look at the site without changing it. New teammates start as
  viewers.

Your role on a site is shown on its page.

## If something goes wrong

- **"… is taken. Try another."**: another site has that address. Pick a
  different one.
- **"You already have a site with that name"**: give this one another name.
- **"You've made as many sites as your account allows"**: you have 5 sites.
- **"Page not found"** on a site's page: you aren't a teammate on that site,
  or it was removed. Ask its admin to invite you.
