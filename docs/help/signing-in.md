# Signing in to Levuche

One Levuche account works on levuche.com and in the app. Sign in on either
one and you're signed in on both.

## Sign in or create your account

1. Open app.levuche.com, or choose **Get started** on levuche.com.
2. Choose **Sign in**.
3. Sign in, or create your account if you're new. You may be asked to confirm
   your email.
4. You come back to the page you opened.

## Your account and security

Choose your name at the top right, then:

- **Account** to change your name, email or photo.
- **Security and devices** to change your password and see the devices
  where you're signed in.

## Sign out

Choose your name at the top right, then **Sign out**. This signs you out of
levuche.com too.

## If something goes wrong

- **"We can't reach the sign-in service right now"**: the page tries again
  by itself. If it keeps showing, try again in a few minutes.
- **"Too many tries"**: wait a minute, then sign in again.
- **You're asked to sign in again**: your session ended, or you signed out
  somewhere else. Sign in again and you'll come back to the same page.
